Privacy Policy

How IKUN Product Publisher processes information for its review-and-publish workflow.

Summary: We use connected TikTok and catalog data to prepare, review, and publish user-approved content. We do not sell personal information.

1. Information we process

  • TikTok basic profile identity needed to identify the connected account.
  • OAuth access and refresh tokens needed to maintain the connection and perform approved actions.
  • Product images selected for a draft and user-reviewed captions.
  • Publishing jobs, approval state, timestamps, and redacted publishing results used to show status and troubleshoot failures.

2. Why we process it

We process this information to connect an account, prepare product-post drafts, display them for review, publish only after explicit approval, show job status, protect the service, and investigate operational errors.

3. How credentials are protected

OAuth tokens are handled server-side and are not placed in public page content. We limit credential access to the publishing functions that require it. The OAuth callback does not display, log, or store an authorization code in browser storage.

4. Sharing and sale

We do not sell personal information. Data is shared only with infrastructure providers needed to operate the service and with TikTok when fulfilling the connected account owner's approved publishing request.

5. Retention and deletion

Connected account data is retained while needed to provide the service. After account removal, tokens and account-linked working data are deleted, subject to limited security and operational logs retained for troubleshooting and integrity.

6. Disconnecting TikTok

An account owner can disconnect TikTok in the product's account settings or revoke the app in TikTok's connected-app settings. Disconnecting stops future API use; the owner may also request deletion of remaining account-linked data through the IKUN support channel associated with the connected business account.

7. Data choices

Account owners can review drafts before publishing, decline a draft, disconnect an account, revoke TikTok access, and request access to or deletion of their service data.

8. Changes

We may update this policy as the controlled beta evolves. The date at the top identifies the current version.

9. Contact

For privacy questions or deletion requests, contact IKUN through the support channel associated with the connected TikTok business account and identify the relevant account username.
